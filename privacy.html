Privacy.

This site is a personal portfolio. It does not run analytics, advertising or third-party trackers, and it sets no cookies.

Nothing automatically. If you use the contact form, the details you type — name, email, an optional subject and your message — are used solely to reply to you.

How the contact form works

Unless a form endpoint is configured, the form opens your own email client with the message pre-filled. In that case nothing is transmitted to this site at all; you send the email yourself.
