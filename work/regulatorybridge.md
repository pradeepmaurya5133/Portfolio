# RegulatoryBridge — Pradeep Maurya

A regulatory compliance platform — dense information that still has to be quick to read.

Canonical: https://pradeepmaurya.com/work/regulatorybridge.html

---

Contributed to website development and improvements for RegulatoryBridge.com, covering responsive design, feature refinement, debugging, testing and performance-focused updates.

Compliance products carry a lot of information per screen, which makes them easy to build badly. The work here was mostly about restraint: tightening layouts so dense tables and long-form requirements stay scannable, and refining existing features rather than stacking new ones on top.

Alongside that I handled debugging and testing passes, and performance-focused updates where pages were doing more work than the content justified.

### Refine over rebuild

Most tickets were improvements to shipped features. Changing a live product without disrupting the people already using it is its own constraint.

### Performance as a feature

On an information-dense platform, how fast a page becomes readable matters as much as what it says.
